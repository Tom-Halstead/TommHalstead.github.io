My Journey

Hi, I’m Tom — a software developer based in Austin, Texas with a strong passion for back-end development and building efficient, scalable applications. Before transitioning into tech, I led teams of technicians in the construction industry, managing the installation of complex conveyance systems for companies like Amazon and UPS. That experience sharpened my problem-solving skills, attention to detail, and ability to adapt quickly — qualities I now bring to every software project I take on.

These days, I find the most excitement in server-side development, API integration, and AWS cloud services. I love diving into complex systems, breaking down challenging problems, and designing clean, maintainable solutions that make a real impact. While my primary focus is on back-end technologies, I also have a solid understanding of front-end development, which allows me to collaborate effectively across the stack and bring ideas to life from end to end.

One of my favorite recent projects is Vita Nova, a web application designed to help users track workouts and personal goals through structured journal entries. The project gave me the opportunity to work with RESTful APIs, implement authentication, and deploy services using AWS — blending problem solving with meaningful functionality.

I’m always eager to take on new challenges, whether that means contributing to a team building a production-grade system or learning a new tool to expand my skill set. I’m currently seeking new opportunities where I can grow as a developer, collaborate with great teams, and build software that solves real problems.
